The Art of Woven Luxury

Our Heritage Story

For over a decade, Royal Affair has stood as a symbol of classical elegance and slow luxury. We build wearable art, celebrating the history of traditional Indian handloom, block printing, and intricate embroidery.

The Genesis

A Vision of Indian Weaves

Royal Affair was founded in 2011 with a simple yet ambitious goal: to revive the grandeur of traditional heritage weaves and translate them into contemporary ethnic silhouettes. Inspired by the royal wardrobes of the Mughal era and the rich textile history of the Indian subcontinent, we began as a small group of custom weavers.

Our founder recognized that true luxury lies in the details—the slight asymmetry of a hand-printed motif, the weight of pure Banarasi silk, and the shimmer of real Zari thread. Today, Royal Affair is headquartered in our private design atelier at the Heritage Mansion, Mall Road, Meerut, Uttar Pradesh, India, bringing custom-fitted masterpieces to clients worldwide.

Every silhouette is handcrafted under direct supervision, respecting old-world methodologies while maintaining modern durability standards.

Corporate Pillars

Mission, Vision & Core Values

Our Mission

To preserve and popularize traditional Indian handloom weaving techniques and block prints. We aim to support master karigars, ensuring sustainable compensation while delivering elite ethnic silhouettes.

Our Vision

To serve as a global ambassador of Indian textile heritage, blending slow fashion sensibilities with clean contemporary lines, making handcraft couture accessible to modern wardrobes.

Brand Values

Uncompromising authenticity, masterly execution, ethical artisan partnerships, and timeless endurance. We refuse synthetic shortcuts, weaving every suit with pure threads.

Wholesale & Custom Tailoring

Whether you require bespoke fittings for a private collection, custom-tailored trousseaus, or bulk wholesale orders for designer boutiques globally, our atelier handles complete design-to-delivery logistics.

Premium Manufacturing

From Loom to Wardrobe

Our manufacturing unit handles custom handloom weaving, chemical-free color dyeing, manual stencil woodblock printing, and dense hand embroideries. We focus primarily on rich fabrics such as Banarasi georgette silk, thick velvet plush, and sheer organza overlays.

By maintaining rigorous quality checkpoints at every step—from drafting structural patterns to setting hand embellishments—each Royal Affair suit emerges as a masterpiece of design and stitching precision.
