1. Information We Collect

We collect contact coordinates (fullname, delivery address, phone, billing email) during order placement, checkout procedures, and account registrations. We also record tailoring preferences, height indicators, and sleeve customization instructions.

2. How We Use Information

We use personal data to manage logistics pipelines, verify pin codes, dispatch package tracking updates, process coupons, and tailor garments to customer specifications. With user consent, we send previews of new collections and private sales.

3. Data Protection & Security

All electronic financial exchanges are processed through secure payment gateways (Razorpay/PayU) with SSL encryption. We do not store raw passwords, credit card numbers, CVVs, or Net Banking PINs on local or storage databases.

4. Cookies & Storage Policies

We utilize local storage configurations to save items inside shopping carts, manage wishlist coordinates, and maintain comparison matrices. Third-party cookies may be used for website analytics and display styling checkups.

5. Third-Party Sharing

We share shipment coordinates with shipping partners (Blue Dart, Delhivery, FedEx) to facilitate deliveries. We do not sell, rent, or distribute personal user information to third-party advertising brokers.

6. Your Data Rights

Registered users have the right to edit their profile settings in the account dashboard, download order history files, request account closures, or withdraw marketing subscriptions at any time. For data deletion inquiries, contact privacy@royalaffair.com.
