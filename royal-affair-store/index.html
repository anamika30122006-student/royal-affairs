Premium Heritage

Uncompromising Craftsmanship

Every Royal Affair designer suit starts as a dream woven in fine Banarasi silk, hand-selected velvet, or breezy organza. Our skilled local karigars bring each motif to life through meticulous Zardozi thread work, traditional Gota Patti stripes, and Kashmiri Aari chain stitchings.

We believe in traditional techniques and modern silhouetting, making sure each wearer experiences the royal ethnic elegance that complements India's rich culture.
